# Governance Pack Reference

## What a Novagentica Governance Pack contains

The governance pack is typically produced at project kickoff and updated at each major milestone. It is a short, client-facing document (not an internal one).

Sections:

1. **Governance Structure** — Who is who, roles and responsibilities (reference RACI)
2. **Meeting & Reporting Cadence** — Recurring touchpoints and their purpose
3. **Escalation Framework** — How issues are raised, tracked, and resolved
4. **Change Control Process** — How scope/timeline/commercial changes are managed
5. **SoW Sign-off Status** — Tracker showing SoW version, date submitted, date signed
6. **Decision Log** — Running log of key decisions made during the engagement
7. **Risk Register (summary)** — Top risks, owner, mitigation, status

---

## Meeting & Reporting Cadence

| Cadence | Format | Attendees | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly status update | Email or 30-min call | NL + CL | Progress, blockers, next steps |
| Milestone review | 60-min meeting | NL + CL + CI | Formal gate: proceed / pause decision |
| Executive touchpoint | 30-min call (as needed) | NS + CS | Escalations, commercial decisions |
| Hypercare daily stand-up | 15-min call | NL + CL | Post-go-live issue tracking |

---

## Escalation Framework

Use this tiered model:

| Tier | Trigger | Escalation To | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Day-to-day issues | NL ↔ CL | Same day |
| Tier 2 | Unresolved after 48h / impacts timeline | NS + CS | 48 hours |
| Tier 3 | Commercial dispute / SoW breach | Legal / Executive | Contractual timeline |

Issues are logged in the Risk Register with status: Open / In Progress / Resolved.

## Governance pack delivery

- Produced as `.docx` in Novagentica brand style
- Shared with client at kickoff — not after
- Versioned: v1.0 at kickoff, updated at each milestone review
- File naming: `Novagentica-[ClientName]-GovernancePack-v1.0.docx`
